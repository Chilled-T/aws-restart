## What I Learned

The **Generative AI for Decision Makers** course introduces the strategic, organizational, and technical considerations necessary for successfully planning and executing Generative AI initiatives. Key areas covered include:

* **Strategic Business Alignment:** Identifying high-impact business opportunities, defining ROI metrics, and evaluating the feasibility of Generative AI adoption across organizational functions.
* **Generative AI Concepts & Terminology:** Understanding foundation models (FMs), Large Language Models (LLMs), diffusion models, fine-tuning, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
* **AWS Generative AI Solutions:** Leveraging enterprise tools such as Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Q, and foundation model ecosystems to drive business transformation.
* **Risk Management & Responsible AI:** Navigating data privacy, intellectual property risks, bias, hallucination mitigation, and ethical AI deployment.
* **Project Lifecycle & Resource Planning:** Structuring GenAI projects from proof-of-concept (PoC) to production, including team capability requirements and cost management.
